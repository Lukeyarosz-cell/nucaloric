# Creative Refresh — October 5, 2026

The founder asked for a whole-site makeover, a stronger creative identity, useful launchpad-inspired features, and removal of striped transitions and gimmicky animations.

## Visual direction

Black `#080809`, paper `#f4f1ec`, pale pink `#e7b5c4`. Supporting grays are neutral opacity/contrast shades. Existing partner logo colors and the original SVG wordmark remain intact. Purple-tinted interface surfaces were normalized. The shared stylesheet is `refresh.css`.

Navigation uses a black vertical chapter curtain with a white wordmark and short destination copy. No diagonal stripes. Existing 560 ms entry and 680 ms exit timing keep it legible. Reduced motion navigates directly.

Motion uses dot topography: horizon waves, arched fields, signal waves, and soft concentric pulses. New fields stop when offscreen or the tab is hidden; reduced motion draws a static composition. The homepage’s accepted bottom wave stays. Rotating Registry sculpture, duplicate ambient engines, animated demonstration cursor, and logo entrance effects were retired.

## What changed

- Home: three creative project kits with distinct dot artwork and direct Studio entry.
- Registry: cream editorial index, black readable rows, neutral status labels, category/search/status filtering, four-capability kits, and an independent add/remove button. All 49 tools remain. The selected toolset persists locally.
- Studio (`studio.html`): a useful working draft with purpose, first milestone, hosting workload, self-reported repo/demo evidence, a proposed builder/community income split, local save, and Markdown export. Text entered by the visitor is rendered safely with textContent.
- Explore: apps, agents, tools, and community lenses over clearly labeled example concepts.
- Launch: direct invitation to plan the project’s useful part in Studio before token parameters.
- Account: saved-brief shortcut; the Paymenter terminal still requires a provisioned backend.
- Shared surfaces: square, quieter panels; neutral colors; removed the hidden drawers’ stray shadow.

## Persistence and limits

`nucCapabilityShortlist`, `nucStudioKit`, and `nucProjectBrief` are browser-local. They are not shared across devices or accounts. Saving reports unavailable storage; exporting still works. No financial transactions, ownership verification, deployments, wallet connection, or revenue payouts were added. The split slider plans future project income and says so in the UI and export.

## Review

Evidence is in `Evidence/Creative Refresh`. Automated checks cover 11 pages at desktop and phone widths, catalog filtering and reset, exact four-tool kits, add/remove behavior, keyboard details/focus restoration, brief persistence, live preview, Markdown export, workload handoff, transitions, and reduced-motion stability. Screenshots document the new Home, Registry, and Studio.

# Product brief
The archive describes a Solana-tech coin site with launch optimization, launchpad tools, rewards, X distribution, ecosystem integrations, and coin exploration. User appeal is a stated priority.

## Intended journeys
1. Explore five sample coins, compare structures, and save a watchlist.
2. Inspect a coin's launch, holders, Mind, treasury, rewards, social activity, and history.
3. Build a launch through Identity, Structure, Liquidity, AI Optimize, Distribution, Mind, Review, and Deploy.
4. Review capabilities and their displayed health, then inspect capability details.
5. Use an account drawer and dashboard for personal activity.

## Current behavior
The journeys are demonstrations. Selecting a wallet stores a provider label locally. Launching and claiming do not perform blockchain operations. Market metrics and registry statuses are embedded examples. See [[Architecture]] and [[Roadmap]].

## Builder platform direction
Support useful Solana projects beyond launch through hosted websites, developer workspaces, scripts, and model workloads. Paymenter is the intended billing layer. See [[Project Hosting]] for current implementation and production dependencies.

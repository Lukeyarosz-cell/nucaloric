# Dark Creative Rebuild

October 5, 2026. Latest founder direction: Status, Studio and Registry had too much white and did not feel like NUCALORIC. This revision supersedes the cream Registry index and cream Studio workspace in [[Creative Refresh]].

## Parameters taken from the vault

[[Design System]], [[Motion Style Guide]], [[Experience Direction]], and [[Future Site Motion]] call for dark surfaces, restrained cream accents, pale pink, a preserved original wordmark, bold readable type, and purposeful motion. The founder's later instruction rejects striped navigation and gimmicky rotating sculptures. Use the dot wave/pulse language of the homepage.

Ink `#080809` is now the default surface. Cream `#f4f1ec` is primarily text. Pink `#e7b5c4` marks selection, headings, signals, and actions. Supporting colors are neutral contrast shades. Partner logos remain original. Manrope is loaded locally for these creative workspace pages; the original SVG wordmark remains unchanged.

## Rebuilt pages

- Status: a signal-wave introduction, four observation counts, a grid for our infrastructure, and paired provider/NUCALORIC panels for each service. The public feeds, source evidence, timestamps, filters, offline states, and honest not-connected labels remain functional.
- Studio: a dark project desk with Idea, Proof, and Plan chapters; a sticky live draft with dot art; and a detail meter tied to entered fields. Chapter links are real section navigation. Saving and Markdown export remain local, with evidence explicitly self-reported.
- Registry: a capability atlas with eight distinct family dot compositions, three purpose kits, numbered ink cards, persistent selected toolset, category/search/status filters, and keyboard-accessible details. All 49 capabilities remain. Prototype usage counters are hidden; service availability links to the dedicated status page.
- Roadmap: inherits the matching dark workspace treatment and motion control to keep the service journey consistent.

## Motion

Weave: two slow crossing ridges. Bloom: a breathing circular dot field. Signal, arch, horizon and ripple continue the accepted dot vocabulary. All canvas surfaces use one shared clock capped at 30 frames per second, device pixel density capped at 1.5, and visibility checks. Work stops offscreen or while the tab is hidden. Reduced motion shows a stable still composition.

A visible pause/resume control stores `nucMotionPaused` locally and follows the visitor across pages. Reduced-motion preferences keep that control disabled. Sections enter once with a short 18px rise; controls stay stable and usable. No stripe wipe, cursor demonstration, tilt loop, or background video was added.
